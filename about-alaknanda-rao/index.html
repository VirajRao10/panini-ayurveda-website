About

Vd. Alaknanda Rao (B.A.M.S., D.Ayu.D., Pune, India)

Alaknanda Rao is an Ayurvedic doctor from India and has been based in the Netherlands for the past 20 years. She has her own practice, and has also been teaching Ayurveda since 1997 to students from the USA, UK, Japan, Belgium, Spain, France, Switzerland, India and The Netherlands and beyond.

Dr. (India) Rao specialises in bringing the authentic knowledge of Ayurveda to the modern world, and tailoring it to fit to the modern lifestyles.
