Consultation flow

How your visit is structured

Ayurvedic guidance

  1. Help you work towards balance in body and mind.
  2. Support your physical and mental wellbeing and vitality.
  3. Identify your body and mind type and develop a personalised plan.
  4. Provide personalised diet and lifestyle advice.
  5. Offer practical guidance for seasonal changes, ageing, stress and challenging life situations.
  6. Discuss natural remedies and supportive approaches for a range of health concerns.

First consultation

  1. Your first consultation includes taking your medical history, a physiological and basic psychological evaluation, including pulse reading, tongue reading and an abdominal examination.
  2. You will receive an Ayurvedic diagnosis based on Prakriti (body type) and Vikruti (current imbalances).
  3. Personalised diet, lifestyle and Ayurvedic supplement advice is provided. You will receive useful information right away; this is more than an intake appointment.
  4. A step-by-step plan will be created, with follow-up consultations scheduled at regular intervals.

Before your appointment

  1. Do not come to your consultation extremely hungry or directly after a large meal. Please do not eat a large meal or drink coffee for 60–90 minutes beforehand.
  2. Schedule your first consultation on a relaxed day and leave some free time afterwards.
  3. When booking appointments for babies and young children, look for a suitable time of the day and avoid nap times. For older children, please explain in simple terms what they can expect.

Areas in which Ayurvedic consultations may offer support

Preventive and lifestyle guidance

  • Optimising health and taking preventive measures

Skin, joints and muscles

  • Eczema, psoriasis and other skin problems

Respiratory, immunity and circulation

Energy, stress and complex support
