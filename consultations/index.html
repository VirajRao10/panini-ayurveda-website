Consultation flow

How your visit is structured

Ayurvedic guidance

  1. Help in finding the balance of your body, mind and soul.
  2. Guide you to improve your physical and mental health, optimizing your vitality.
  3. Determine your body and mind type, and develop an improvement strategy based on these.
  4. Provide guided diet and lifestyle advice.
  5. Give clear directions to keep fit in changing seasons, age, stress and challenging life situations.
  6. Discuss natural remedies and supportive approaches for various diseases.

First consultation

  1. Your first consultation will involve extensive physical examination and basic psychological evaluation, including pulse, tongue and abdominal palpation examination and your medical history.
  2. An advice and diet plan for you will be developed.
  3. There will be a diagnosis made based on Prakriti (body type) and Vikruti (current problems). Guidelines for diet, life style and potential instruction for Ayurvedic supplements will be given to you.
  4. Follow up appointments will be required at regular intervals depending on the plan of action.

Before your appointment

  1. Do not come to your consultation extremely hungry, or directly after a large meal. Please do not eat or drink coffee for 60-90 minutes before.
  2. Make your first appointment on a relaxed day with no close appointments after.
  3. When booking appointments for babies and young children, look for a suitable time of the day and avoid nap times. For older children, please explain in simple terms what they can expect.
