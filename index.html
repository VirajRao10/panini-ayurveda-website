About us

Panini Ayurveda Est. 1992

Senior Ayurvedic Consultant Alaknanda Rao (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) studied full-time at Pune University for five and a half years and passed with distinction in two subjects. Alongside her university education, she worked with many renowned Vaidyas and gained practical experience. After a one-year internship in urban and rural hospitals, she received her licence to practise Ayurveda.

In 1992, she opened a small city-based clinic in Pune. It was named after Panini, the ancient scholar of Ayurveda, Yoga and Sanskrit. After more than 10 successful years in practice, she completed a postgraduate qualification in Ayurvedic Dietetics at Tilak Maharashtra University in Pune, India.

Alaknanda Rao began working in Europe in 2002. Panini Ayurveda is now based primarily in Amstelveen, the Netherlands.

Ayushmat Ayurveda Clinic in Pune remains active, offering personalised Panchakarma treatments and internship programmes for Ayurveda students.
